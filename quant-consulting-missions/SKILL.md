## Pitfalls

- Expert networks pay well but project volume is inconsistent
- Freelance marketplaces have race-to-the-bottom pricing on lower tiers
- LinkedIn "remote" jobs often still require US/EU timezone overlap
- Crypto jobs may pay in volatile tokens
- Always verify client legitimacy before sharing proprietary methods
- Keep signal logic confidential per Aqfinea's value prop
